📖 Kaggriculture Rules & Gameplay Cheatsheet

🌱 Crops

  • Wheat ($10): Harvest days 2-4, bonus watering days 2-4. Feed for livestock.
  • Carrot ($20): Harvest days 2-3, fast early game capital.
  • Tomato ($50): Day 8+, repeatable every 1 turn, Ongoing crop.
  • Strawberry ($100): Day 10+, repeatable every 2 turns, high revenue factory.
  • Melon ($80): Days 10-12, single large harvest with high base value ($250).

🐄 Livestock & Animals

  • Goose ($300): Requires COOP structure, produces Eggs daily.
  • Cow ($400): Requires PASTURE structure, produces Milk every 2 days.
  • Sheep ($500): Requires PASTURE structure, produces Wool every 3 days.
  • Must be fed Wheat daily. CARE banks +1 product bonus per fed day.

🗺️ Farmland & Farmhands

  • Four 5x5 quadrants: Starts with NW unlocked. Buy NE ($1k), SW ($2k), SE ($4k).
  • Hire Farmhands via Fibonacci costs ($1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8...). Resets daily.
  • Shed holds max 100 items. Stand at (4,4), (5,4), (4,5), (5,5) for center storage.

📈 Market Dynamics & Town

  • Sale prices fluctuate with supply and demand. Oversupply drives prices to $1 floor.
  • Town Center & unlocking Town Shops create continuous consumption demand.
  • Drip sell harvested goods steadily, and liquidate 100% on days 28-29.
